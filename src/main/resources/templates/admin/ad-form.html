How to get AdSense IDs
  1. Sign in to your Google AdSense account
  2. Go to "Ads" → "Overview"
  3. Click "By ad unit" and create a new ad unit
  4. Copy the Publisher ID (ca-pub-xxxxx) and Ad Slot ID
  5. Paste them into the form above
